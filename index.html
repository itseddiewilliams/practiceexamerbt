How to Study Using a Practice Exam With Explanations

1. Take the Exam Before Studying

Get a true baseline score before reviewing any explanations. This shows where you naturally stand.

2. Review Every Explanation Carefully

Even if you answered correctly-read the rationale! Each explanation reinforces how the concepts work.

3. Highlight Weak Areas

Use a notebook or digital notes to list topics you consistently get wrong. Examples may include:

  • ABC data

  • Prompt fading

  • Extinction procedures

  • Ethical reporting rules

4. Create a Study Routine Around Mistakes

Your practice exam results should guide your study schedule. Study weak topics first, then take another exam to measure improvement.

5. Retake the Exam After Reviewing Notes

Repetition is key. Seeing the same concept again helps reinforce your understanding.

6. Mimic Real Testing Conditions

To build confidence, practice in a quiet room, set time limits, and avoid distractions.
